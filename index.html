● Features

Everything you need
to stay productive

Vault packs powerful tools into a beautiful, distraction-free interface that helps you get more done every day.

📊

Daily Stats Dashboard

Track blocked time, yesterday's performance, and today's task count at a glance with colorful metric cards and a productivity score out of 100.

🎯

Focus Sessions

Timed focus mode with a circular countdown timer. Set your duration, block distracting apps, and lock in on what matters most.

📅

Calendar View

Browse tasks by date with a swipeable date strip. Each task shows its emoji, priority, and a quick-start button to begin immediately.

🔓

App Blocking

Block social media, games, and other distractions during focus sessions. Choose specific apps or use quick-block categories to stay in the zone.

📈

Productivity Score

Get a daily productivity score with a visual ring gauge. Track your screen time, app usage breakdown, and see how you're trending over time.

🔍

Smart Search & Filters

Find any task instantly with powerful search. Filter by priority, status, tags, or date range to surface exactly what you need.

● How It Works

Get started in seconds

Three simple steps to take control of your day.

1

Create your tasks

Add tasks with a custom emoji, priority level, due date, duration, and tags. Optionally block apps for each task.

2

Enter focus mode

Start a timed focus session with the 25-minute Pomodoro timer. Block distracting apps and get in the zone.

3

Track your score

Check your Daily Stats dashboard to see your productivity score, screen time chart, and app usage breakdown.
