Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Vault is a productivity app for iOS and Android. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our app.

2. Information We Collect

All data is stored locally on your device and never leaves it. Vault does not have servers, user accounts, or cloud sync. The types of data stored on your device include:

  • Task data — titles, descriptions, due dates, priorities, tags, and emoji
  • Focus session history — duration, completion status, and timestamps
  • Profile information — display name and avatar photo
  • Productivity data — scores, streaks, and daily statistics
  • App usage data — read from the operating system to calculate your productivity score; never transmitted off your device

3. Device Permissions We Use

Vault requests certain device permissions to provide its core features. Here is what each permission is used for:

  • Screen Time API / Family Controls (iOS) — Shields distracting apps during focus sessions. Vault requests access to managed settings; no Screen Time data is read or stored by Vault beyond the shield/unshield action.
  • Usage Access (Android) — Reads app usage statistics to calculate your productivity score and screen time charts. This data is processed on-device only.
  • Display Over Other Apps / Overlay (Android) — Shows a shielding overlay when you open a shielded app during a focus session.
  • Notifications (iOS & Android) — Sends reminders for upcoming tasks and focus session completions.
  • Photo Library (iOS & Android) — Allows you to select a profile avatar image. The image is stored locally.

4. Data Sharing

We do not transmit, sell, or share any of your data with third parties. There is no server, no account system, and no cloud sync. Your data exists only on your device.

5. Analytics

Vault does not currently collect any analytics or usage telemetry. If anonymous, privacy-respecting analytics are added in a future update, this section will be updated to describe exactly what is collected and how to opt out.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Your data persists on your device until you uninstall the app or clear app data through your device settings. There is no cloud backup; once deleted, data cannot be recovered.

7. Children's Privacy

Vault is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes.

9. Contact Us

For privacy questions or concerns, please contact us at misterphrogg@gmail.com.
